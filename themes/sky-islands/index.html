A little about me

Hi, I'm Ekansh.

By day I work in finance — careful work where every number has to add up.

In the evenings I build small, useful things: a way to share parking, an honest index of car problems, and simple QR menus for cafés. I don't write code. I sketch, decide, and work with AI tools until the idea in my head exists in the world.

Off the clock: long trips, a book most nights, and wondering what to build next.

Parking, shared

Parkly

A marketplace that helps drivers find private parking spots — plus a QR sticker that lets anyone alert a car's owner without ever seeing their phone number.

I look after all of it: the idea, the look, and getting it out into the world. None of it is hand-coded — I describe it, AI tools build it, and I keep refining until it's right.

Car problems, ranked

CarItch

Every car has its little itch — a rattle, a recall, a service request that never gets a reply. CarItch gathers those complaints and ranks them by how serious owners say they are, so everyone sees the honest picture.

I run it end to end — the research, the writing and the publishing.

Live · started April 2026

Menus that report back

Platro

Scan a QR code at the table to browse the menu, order, and collect loyalty rewards — no app to install. Made for small cafés that don't need anything complicated.

Owners get one simple dashboard for menu changes and sales; guests get a menu that just works.

Live · started July 2026
